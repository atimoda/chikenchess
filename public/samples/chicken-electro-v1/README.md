# Chicken Electro 01

Original electronic samples made for ChickenChess; generated reproducibly by
`scripts/generate-samples.py`. No third-party recordings or melodies are included.
Audio files: CC0-1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
32 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM. Bell reference pitch: C4 (261.6255653 Hz).
Variants represent timbral articulations; musical accents remain configurable.
These samples are usable by any piece, independently of its chess role.
