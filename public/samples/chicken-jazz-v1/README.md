# Chicken Jazz · 01

Original synthetic one-shots generated by `scripts/generate-jazz-samples.py`.
Audio assets dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0.

Reed, baritone, brass and vowel-like tones are synthesis approximations, not
recordings of acoustic instruments or a singer. No audio was extracted from
Mr. Scruff, Moondog or T-Bone Walker recordings. Roots: reed/brass/formant/keys
C4, baritone C3, pluck C2. Mono PCM16, 32 kHz; buffers load on demand and are shared.
